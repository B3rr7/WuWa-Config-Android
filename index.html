11 / Tech & Deploy

Under the Hood

How deployment stays safe, and what the app is built with.

Auto-Tune state machine
IDLE→DEPLOYING→WAITING_FOR_PLAY→CAPTURING→COMPLETE

FPS < target ×0.85 → step DOWN a preset · ≥15% headroom → step UP · progressive cuts by gap: SSR (>15) → bloom (>10) → radial blur (>8) → shadows (>5). Order: Cinematic → Ultra → High → Balanced → Competitive → Endurance → Performance → Potato.

Deploy safety system
D.01

Hash Sync

KuroConfigMonitor snapshots hashes before deploy and reconciles after — concurrent game access is detected. ModifyCount capped at 8; atomic .new → rename push with read-back verification.

D.02

Deploy History

deploy_history.json stores up to 20 records: preset, method, files, accepted / redundant / unknown counts, plus baseline & outcome metrics (FPS, thermal, OOM, drops) for delta comparison.

D.03

Optimizer Retune

Stable (FPS ±5, no OOM): keep. Degraded: screen →75%, shadow −2, detail −1. OOM event: jump to Potato baseline. Improved: screen ×1.15, shadow +1.

Built with
Kotlin 1.9.24 Jetpack Compose BOM 2024.10 MVVM + StateFlow Material 3 Navigation Compose AGP 8.4.2 Gradle 8.6 SDK 26+ / Target 34 Gson Coil ExoPlayer JUnit 4 ktlint ProGuard

12 / FAQ

Before You Ask

Who made this? Are you a developer?
A player, like you — not a professional dev. I faced the same config pain, planned a tool, and built it. It's open source, so you can check every line, and every issue you report becomes the next fix.
What is WuWaConfig?
A free Android app that optimizes Wuthering Waves. It reads your Client.log, scores your phone with SmartBrain (0–100), generates optimized Engine.ini, Scalability.ini and GameUserSettings.ini, then deploys via ADB, Shizuku, Root or SAF.
Is it stable? What if something breaks?
It's actively evolving — most devices work great, and edge cases get fixed fast because it's open source. Safety nets are built in either way: auto-backup before every deploy, one-tap restore, and hash verification. Worst case, you're one tap from your original config.
How do I boost FPS without root?
Use ADB Wireless Debugging or Shizuku — both work on stock, non-rooted phones. SmartBrain reads your real hardware (GPU tier, RAM, Vulkan, thermal history) and picks the right preset. Flagships can unlock 120 FPS; mid-rangers gain stability and fewer drops rather than a huge raw-FPS jump.
Is it safe and private?
Yes. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting — nothing leaves your device except user-initiated gacha API calls. android:allowBackup is disabled and ADB keys are encrypted with AndroidKeyStore (AES-256-GCM).
Which phones are supported?
Any Android 8+ phone — Snapdragon (Adreno), Dimensity (Mali), Exynos, Tensor, Kirin, Helio — including low-end. Per-chipset DeviceProfiles cover Poco, Redmi, Xiaomi, Samsung, OnePlus, Realme, Vivo, Oppo, Nothing, Motorola, Asus ROG and more.
Does it work on Samsung Galaxy S23 / S24?
Yes — both Snapdragon and Exynos variants, on Android 8+. Connect via Shizuku or ADB Wireless Debugging, pick a preset, deploy. Exynos models should start one tier lower than the Snapdragon equivalent for thermal headroom.
Does it optimize MediaTek Dimensity phones with a Mali GPU?
Yes — per-chipset tuning for Dimensity (Mali/PowerVR) is built in. Mali GPUs benefit most from lower shadow quality and reduced texture streaming, so Competitive or Balanced is usually the sweet spot; SmartBrain scores your exact GPU tier automatically.
Best preset for Poco X6 Pro / Snapdragon?
Start with Balanced or High and let SmartBrain tune. Snapdragon 8 Gen 2/3 flagships can run Ultra or Cinematic with the 120 FPS unlock; mid-range chips are smoothest on Competitive or Performance.
Best settings for Redmi Note 12/13 & budget phones?
Start with Performance or Endurance — conservative render scale and shadows avoid thermal throttling and GPU OOM while still beating stock FPS.
How do I get 120 FPS?
Enable the 120 FPS unlock toggle — it raises the frame-pace cap in GameUserSettings.ini. Works on capable flagships. If you overheat or drop frames, step down a preset until stable.
